Pizza Sales Analysis Project - Requirements


I. Business Context

You are a Data Analyst for a pizza manufacturing and distribution company. Your task is to analyze historical sales data to generate actionable insights that will improve business operations. The dataset provided is highly denormalized and raw.

Provided Files:
- data_pizza.csv: Raw transactional records for the year 2015.
- data_dictionary.xlsx: Describes the data fields within the transaction table.


II. Project Tasks & Grading Criteria (Maximum Score: 5.0 points)

1. Data Transformation (Power Query) - (0.50 pts)
The data source is provided as a denormalized flat file. Use Power Query Editor to clean and restructure the data:
a. Ensure correct data types for all columns.
b. Transform queries and values as needed.
c. Normalize the data source into Fact and Dim tables. For Dim tables: keep relevant columns, remove duplicates on the whole table to ensure uniqueness, and create a primary key. For Fact table: merge with Dim tables using left join, expand to map foreign keys, and remove redundant attributes.
Rule: Import the raw data directly into Power BI without altering the data source beforehand.

2. Data Modeling - (0.25 pts)
Build a relational data model (star or snowflake schema):
a. Establish active relationships with proper cardinality and filter direction between dimension and fact tables.
b. Create a dedicated Date dimension table (via DAX or Power Query) to support time intelligence functions.
c. Hide unnecessary fields in the Fact table in report view to prevent users from filtering using invalid fields and reduce clutter.

3. Core KPIs & Measures (DAX) - (0.5 pts)
Create explicit DAX measures and display them using KPI Card visuals:
a. Total Revenue
b. Total Quantity Sold
c. Total Orders
d. Average Order Value
e. Average Pizzas per Order

4. Sales Trends and Anomalies - (0.75 pts)
a. Time Analysis: Analyze and present daily, weekly, and monthly sales trends across 2015.
b. Day/Time Analysis: Investigate sales performance by day of the week and hour of the day based on Total Orders.
c. Anomaly Detection: Highlight and investigate the potential causes behind significant sales spikes or drops on notable dates.

5. Product & Size Analysis - (0.75 pts)
a. Rankings: Rank pizza categories by Total Quantity Sold and Total Revenue.
b. Top/Bottom Performers: Identify the top 5 and bottom 5 specific pizzas by name based on both Total Quantity Sold and Total Revenue.
c. Size Distribution: Analyze the relationship between pizza size and Total Quantity Sold within each pizza category. Ensure pizza sizes are sorted in their proper logical order (S, M, L, XL, XXL).

6. Ingredient & Correlation Analysis - (0.5 pts)
a. Ingredient Popularity: Identify the most and least popular individual ingredients (hint: splitting comma-separated strings will be required) for each pizza category based on Total Quantity Sold.
b. Price vs. Volume: Explore the correlation between average pizza prices and Total Quantity Sold across categories.

7. Order Frequency & Timing - (0.5 pts)
a. Order Size Habit: Analyze the frequency of single-item orders versus multi-item orders.
b. Turnaround Time: Compute and display the average time interval (in minutes) between consecutive orders sorted chronologically.

8. Visual Presentation & UI/UX Design - (0.75 pts)
a. Ensure clean layout, professional dashboard formatting, and cohesive color themes.
b. Use appropriate unit formatting and rounding across all visuals.
c. Use intuitive slicers, include clear page navigation, descriptive chart titles, and clean tooltips.

9. Documentation of Analyses, Insights & Recommendations - (0.5 pts)
Document the analysis logic, business insights, and actionable recommendations.

---

III. Deliverables

Submit the following:
1. A single .pbix file containing the fully modeled data, DAX measures, and interactive dashboard visuals.
2. Documentation of business findings and recommendations either within the dashboard (using text boxes/dedicated pages) or as a separate PDF file.
